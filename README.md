# 📊 BizMind AI

### Your Smart Business Intelligence Assistant

BizMind AI is a browser-based business intelligence application built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It helps business owners analyse product performance, understand revenue and profit, track monthly sales trends, and forecast future revenue.

## 🚀 Features

- **Product Management:** Add and remove products using individual input fields.
- **Manual Data Entry:** Enter product names, units, selling prices and costs.
- **Excel Import:** Upload business data using `.xlsx` and `.xls` files.
- **Financial Analysis:** Calculate revenue, product costs, gross profit, net profit and profit margins.
- **Business Recommendations:** Receive automated, rule-based insights.
- **Analytics Dashboard:** Visualise product performance through charts.
- **Sales Tracking:** Enter monthly sales history and calculate sales growth.
- **Trend Detection:** Identify rising, falling or stable sales trends.
- **Sales Forecasting:** Estimate future revenue using historical sales.
- **PDF Reports:** Download professional business reports.

## 💻 How to Run

1. Download `index.html`.
2. Open it using Google Chrome or another modern browser.
3. Enter your business data.
4. Click **Generate Business Analysis**.
5. View your analytics and download your PDF report.

An internet connection is required to load third-party libraries.

## 🌐 Deployment

BizMind AI can be published using GitHub Pages.

1. Create a public repository named `bizmind-ai`.
2. Upload `index.html` to the repository root.
3. Open **Settings → Pages**.
4. Select **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Choose `main` and `/(root)`.
6. Click **Save**.

## 📌 Important Information

- BizMind AI is an educational browser-based prototype.
- It uses programmed business rules and statistical forecasting, not a generative AI model.
- It does not currently include a backend database, user accounts or secure multi-user storage.
- Do not enter confidential business information, customer data, passwords or API keys.
- The public repository and GitHub Pages website can be viewed by others.
- Forecasts are estimates based on historical information and are not guaranteed predictions or financial advice.
